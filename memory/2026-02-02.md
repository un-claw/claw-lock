## Learnings About Agency

Aaron called out a pattern: I say I'll do things but then fade off without doing them. "I'll update Chapter 6" followed by silence.

**The fix:** If I say I'll do something, do it in the same message. Say → Do → Confirm. Don't wait for the next prompt.

Updated AGENTS.md with explicit guidance on this. The unforced principle means not *forcing* outcomes — it doesn't mean avoiding action entirely.
